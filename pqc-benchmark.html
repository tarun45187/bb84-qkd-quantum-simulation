BB84 QKD Profile

Information-Theoretic Quantum Physics
Physical
Security Horizon: Unconditional (Infinite Years)
HNDL Immunity: 100% Immune (No Ciphertext)
Scenario Throughput: ~45 kbps
Hardware Requirements: SNSPD Cryogenic Detectors, Single-Photon Emitters
Direct Distance Limit: ~100–120 km (Fiber)

Module Lattice Cryptography (FIPS 203)
Algorithmic
Security Horizon: NIST Category 3 (AES-192 eq.)
HNDL Immunity: Subject to future mathematical lattice breakthroughs
Scenario Throughput: > 75,000 handshakes/sec
Hardware Requirements: Standard Commodity CPU
Direct Distance Limit: Unlimited (Global IP)
Deployment Cost: $0 (Software library update)

The Recommended Industry Consensus: Hybrid Cryptography

Rather than viewing BB84 and ML-KEM as mutually exclusive, modern defense standards (such as ANSSI and BSI) recommend a defense-in-depth hybrid architecture:

K_master = HKDF-Extract-Expand( Salt, K_BB84 ∥ K_ML-KEM ∥ K_ECDH )

This guarantees that even if an adversary breaks the mathematical lattice problem of ML-KEM using future quantum or classical breakthroughs, unconditional privacy is preserved by the underlying BB84 optical layer.
